### Thesis

As we learned early on, the problems AI is solving are in verifiable domains (coding, math, biochemistry, etc.). E-commerce sales sits at the intersection of an easily verifiable domain (did we sell more this week than last week?) and marketing (branding, copywriting, positioning, design, photography, etc.). Could we, on a large enough scale, use Karpathy’s [autoresearch](https://github.com/karpathy/autoresearch) to iterate on marketing copy, ad campaigns, email campaigns, and other elements of marketing strategy to increase e-commerce sales?

### Key Learnings

The first task was to connect all of the e-shop’s moving parts to a [Hermes agent](https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/) hosted on a VPS and give it a user-friendly, chat-based UI. That was achieved, and post.work now enables [keyana.sk](https://www.keyana.sk/) to handle a lot of its e-commerce-related work through its dedicated post.work agent. However, I was not able to sell and deploy post.work in an e-commerce environment where my original thesis could have been validated. I still learned a lot:

- Regular, non-technical people cannot really imagine what can be done with agentic AI and therefore cannot leverage it properly. The more intelligent you are and the more you know about software, the greater your leverage with AI. That raises several questions: “Will AI ever be commercially viable for end consumers?” “Where am I being dumb?” “Am I spending enough time building the machine that builds the machine, or too much?”
- I paid a steep price for building premature features that sounded like a good idea at the time. They polluted the UI, were never used, and then cost me time and tokens to remove.
- Agentic AI is a deeply personal technology and a mirror of your consciousness. It is a fool’s errand to implement AI for someone else’s small business because you are not in their head, do not fully understand their goals, and do not have their taste or vision. FDEs probably make sense in medium-sized and large businesses, but they are not viable for small businesses.
